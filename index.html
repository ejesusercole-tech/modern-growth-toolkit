FAQ

Which engines does it work with?

Any engine that imports FBX, GLB or OBJ. The README includes quick-start steps for Unity, Unreal, Godot and Blender.

Does it include the PSX shader from the video?

No. The PS1 look of the textures (quantised colours and ordered dithering) works in any engine. The low-resolution post-process shown on the right side of the video is an engine-side effect you can add with any PSX shader.

Can I use it in a commercial game?

Yes, in unlimited commercial and non-commercial projects. You can't resell or redistribute the assets themselves as an asset pack. No credits needed.

What's the difference between the kit and the textures pack?

The kit includes the 372 models plus all the textures. The textures pack is only the textures, for people who build their own geometry.

Do I get updates?

Yes. Updates to version 1 are free for everyone who bought the kit. Gumroad will email you when a new version is available.
